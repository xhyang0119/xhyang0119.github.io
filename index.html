About me

I am a Ph.D. student in mathematics at Peking University, based at the Beijing International Center for Mathematical Research (BICMR).

My mathematical interests lie in representation theory and automorphic forms, with a particular interest in the Langlands program.

Research interests

  • Representation theory

    Reductive groups over local fields and the representation theory of p-adic groups.

  • Automorphic forms

    The trace formula, endoscopy, and the interplay between local and global questions.

  • The Langlands program

    Connections between representations, arithmetic, and geometry.
